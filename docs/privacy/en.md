# ToddlerLogic Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

ToddlerLogic is a game app for young children. It is designed to be used by a child together with an adult, with no accounts, no ads and no internet connection.

## Summary

- ToddlerLogic **does not collect, send or share any data**, neither the child's nor the adult's.
- The app does not connect to the internet: it has no permission to do so.
- There are no ads, analytics, remote crash reporting or third-party services.
- The little the app remembers is stored only on this phone.

## What is stored on the phone

So that the game continues where it was left, the app stores in the phone's private storage:

- The level reached in each game, the stars, the badges and the date of the last game.
- The Parents' Zone settings: language and sound.
- Whether the Premium version is unlocked (for when payment exists).

No names, photos, voice, location, contacts or device or advertising identifiers are stored.

## Where that data is and how to delete it

- The data never leaves the phone. The app turns off cloud backup and transfer to another phone, so it is not copied to Google or to the phone maker either.
- It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in *Settings › Apps › ToddlerLogic › Storage*.
- Since nobody else has that data, there is nothing to request, correct or delete on any server.

## Permissions

The app does not ask for any permission: it does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts, files or the internet.

## Children

ToddlerLogic is aimed at children aged 8 and under and follows the requirements of Google Play's Designed for Families program. Because it collects no personal data, there is no children's data to protect, sell or share, in line with COPPA (United States), the GDPR (European Union) and the Children's Code (United Kingdom).

The app settings are in the Parents' Zone, protected by an adult challenge.

## Purchases

For now the app is free and has no purchases. If a one-time payment through Google Play is added in the future, Google Play will handle the payment and this policy will be updated before it is released.

## Feedback

In the Parents' Zone, "Send feedback" opens the phone's email app with a prepared, **unsent** email. At the end it includes the app version, the Android version, the phone model and the app language, to help us understand a problem; nothing about the child or their progress. The adult sees everything in it, can change or delete it, and decides whether to send it: the app sends nothing by itself.

If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you write. We only use it to reply to you and we do not share it with anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the new version will be published here with its date and included in the next app update.
